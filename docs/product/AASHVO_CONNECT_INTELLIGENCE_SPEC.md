# AASHVO Connect Intelligence — Product Specification

## Executive Overview
AASHVO Connect Intelligence transforms flatmate discovery from naive superficial filtering into a multi-dimensional, location-aware, timeline-aligned compatibility matching engine. It pairs roommates across 7 deterministic lifestyle dimensions, empowers members to personalize their priority weights, explains matches with zero hallucination, and preserves mutual consent safety standards.

---

## 1. Key Value Propositions
1. **7-Dimension Deterministic Scoring**:
   - Budget Overlap (Default 20% weight)
   - Location & Neighborhood Proximity (Default 20% weight)
   - Cleanliness & Living Habits (Default 20% weight)
   - Food & Dietary Alignment (Default 15% weight)
   - Sleep Routine & Circadian Sync (Default 10% weight)
   - Move-in Date & Flexibility (Default 10% weight)
   - Shared Hobbies & Interests (Default 5% weight)

2. **Personalized Preference Weights**:
   - Members can tune individual dimension weights via interactive sliders.
   - Live relative normalization ensures all weights sum cleanly to 100%.
   - Defaults are instantly restorable with single-click reset.

3. **Data-Driven "Why You Match" Explanations**:
   - Explanations are derived strictly from concrete verified profile attributes (e.g., exact locality match, budget overlap range, diet alignment, move-in gap).
   - Zero hallucinations or arbitrary claims.

4. **Advanced Discovery Filters & Sorting**:
   - Filter candidates by locality, budget range, room type, target move-in date, diet, sleep schedule, cleanliness, and lifestyle flags.
   - Sort candidates by Best Compatibility Score, Budget (Low to High), or Budget (High to Low).

5. **Mutual Consent Safety & Co-Living Exclusivity**:
   - Direct messaging unlocks solely upon mutual double-opt-in interest.
   - Unauthenticated visitors are gated with an onboarding prompt.
   - Co-living and flatmate search only; romantic solicitations prohibited.
